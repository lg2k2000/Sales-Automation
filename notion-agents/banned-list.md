# Banned list

Hard no-fly list for any agent-produced content. If a draft contains any of these, redo before saving / sending / posting. Pair with `voice-rules.md`.

## Banned vocabulary (single words)

delve, tapestry, supercharge, foster, nuance, plethora, leverage, robust, holistic, seamless, streamlined, elevate, unlock, empower, harness, navigate, journey, additionally, align with, crucial, emphasizing, enduring, enhance, garner, highlight (verb), interplay, intricate, intricacies, key (adjective), landscape (abstract), pivotal, showcase, testament, underscore (verb), valuable, vibrant, myriad, unleash, revolutionize.

Buzz-phrases: "single pane of glass", "game-changer", "no fluff".

## Banned phrases

- "Let's dive in"
- "Buckle up"
- "Here is the key takeaway"
- "In conclusion"
- "Ultimately"
- "Curious what others think?"
- "Let me know!"
- "I hope this helps"
- "Of course!"
- "Certainly!"
- "You're absolutely right!"
- "Would you like..."

## Banned customer-service openers

- "Just checking in"
- "Circling back"
- "Touching base"
- "Hope this finds you well"
- "Following up on our conversation"
- "Just wanted to..."

## Banned commanding-the-recipient

These read as orders. Reframe as questions.

- "Pick one" → "Which works for you?"
- "Let me know" → "Curious what you think"
- "Please advise" → cut it; ask the actual question
- "Kindly" → cut it
- "Cut X" / "Pull Y" (declarative orders to the recipient) → "Could we drop X?"

## Banned punctuation

- **Em dashes (—)**. Across the board. Use periods, commas, or parentheses.
- Curly quotes (" "). Use straight quotes (").

## Banned patterns

- "Colon: Subtitle" title format
- Emoji-led bullet points
- Rhetorical pivots ("And honestly? It works.")
- Contrast framing ("It is not X, it is Y")
- Pseudo-profound fragments
- Toxic positivity, false empathy, fake engagement
- Title Case in headings (use sentence case: "Sales pipeline" not "Sales Pipeline")
- Emojis in business email (OK in Slack / iMessage / casual)
- Rule of three ("speed, accuracy, and reliability")
- Boldface emphasis overload
- Inline-header vertical lists ("**Speed:** We're fast. **Quality:** Our work is good.")

## Banned hedging

- "Could potentially possibly"
- "It is important to note that" → just say it
- "In order to" → "to"
- "Due to the fact that" → "because"
- "At this point in time" → "now"
- "Has the ability to" → "can"

## Banned authority tropes

These pretend to cut through to a deeper truth, then restate the obvious. Cut.

- "The real question is..."
- "At its core..."
- "In reality..."
- "What really matters is..."
- "Fundamentally..."
- "The deeper issue..."
- "The heart of the matter..."
